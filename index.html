A note on the shop

Waller's is the kind of shop a city keeps. A counter, a window onto Victoria Avenue, the day's beef ranged behind glass, and a small queue out the door on Saturday morning.

We work from a short list of trusted East Anglian farms. Beef hangs until it's ready. Sausages are made on the premises. The pies are cooked here, in the back, by hand, in the morning.

If you'd like something cut a particular way — a crown, a rolled shoulder, a fore-rib for Sunday — phone the shop. We'll have it ready.
